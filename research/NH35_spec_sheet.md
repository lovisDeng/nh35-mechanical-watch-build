# Seiko NH35 Mechanical Movement Technical Reference

This document compiles technical research and baseline engineering parameters for the Seiko NH35 automatic mechanical movement used as the core subsystem for this wristwatch build.

##  Technical Specifications & Constraints
*   **Dimensions:** Main casing diameter of 27.40mm; casing height/thickness of 5.32mm.
*   **Stem Height:** 1.92mm (critical metric for aligning the winding stem hole in the case with the movement clutch system).
*   **Beat Rate:** 21,600 vibrations per hour (vph) / 3 Hz frequency, producing a sweeping seconds hand motion.
*   **Jewel Count:** 24 jewels integrated at pivot friction points to control physical wear across the gear train.
*   **Escapement System:** Traditional balance wheel with hairspring regulator mechanism.
*   **Winding Efficiency:** Bi-directional pawl winding system ("Magic Lever" mechanism) utilizing kinetic energy from a rotating heavy mass rotor.
*   **Power Reserve:** ~41 hours maximum capacity when fully tensioned via the mainspring barrel.

##  Hand Pinion Dimensions (Tolerance Interface)
To prevent friction and binding, hand selection must match these exact pinion diameter tolerances:
*   **Hour Hand Pinion:** 1.50mm
*   **Minute Hand Pinion:** 1.00mm
*   **Seconds Hand Pinion:** 0.21mm
